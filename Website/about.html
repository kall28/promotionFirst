Cinépolis was founded in Mexico in 1971. Currently, Cinépolis has positioned itself as the second largest cinema network in the world in terms of audiences, operating in the 691 cinema complex with 5.609 screens and entertaining over 338 million audiences annually with a global presence in 17 countries.
Cinépolis was the world’s first cinema network to introduce the first luxury cinema concept in 1999 in Mexico City. Cinépolis aims to give audiences the best experience in watching entertainment and has manpower of more than 39.185 people worldwide to support the company's mission.
