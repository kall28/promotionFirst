Starring Kalian Pantas Mati directed by Ginanti Rona, JKT 48 Zee made her film debut. This was her first experience starring in a movie as the main character with Emir Mahira. “This is my first experience getting into acting. I have never had any experience before. The role I play is great so that I can learn many things,” said JKT 48 Zee.

Besides Zee and Emir Mahira, this film is also starred by Andrew Barret, Angel Sianturi, Farandika, Shatora Narajan, Kezia Caroline, Chelcy Clarissa, Gaby Warouw, Neysa Chandria, Iszur Muchtar, Randhika Djamil, Ariyo Wahab, and Jenny Zhang.

This film is produced by Ideosource Entertainment and Paragon Pictures in collaboration with a movie production from South Korea, Newko Global Entertainment.

Kalian Pantas Mati tells the story of Rakka (Emir Mahira), a male adolescent with a sixth sense. He can communicate with dead souls.

However, his ability causes him to be bullied by his friends. Thus, Rakka decides to move from Jakarta to Bogor.

Are you curious about the next story? Watch Kalian Pantas Mati at Cinepolis on October 13, 2022.
