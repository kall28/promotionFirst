What is Cinépolis Macro XE?

Macro XE is a particular theater with a giant screen format exclusively available at Cinépolis. With an ultra-wide 25-meter screen, double-layer projection system, and extraordinary digital audio system, moviegoers can experience the depth of the latest movie that has never been before. Several Macro XE theaters are equipped with Dolby Atmos technology for the most immersive sound experience
